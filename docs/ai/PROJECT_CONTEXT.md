# PROJECT CONTEXT — Lambrín POS & Platform

## 1. Business Overview
The project is a Point of Sale (POS) and inventory management system designed for **Lambrín** (decorative wall panels). Lambrín products are sold in panels/pieces, boxes, or square meters, featuring multi-level categorization, custom pricing, wholesale pricing tiers, layaways, advance payments, delivery tracking, and immutable audit logs.

## 2. Project Phases
- **Phase 1**: Internal POS system (Login, Employees, Roles/Permissions, POS Panel, Customers, Products, Inventory, Sales, Layaway/Advances, Payments, Document Support, Promotions, Quotes, Shipping, Bitácora, Discounts, Cash Shifts & Cuts, PDF/Excel Reports, USB Barcode Scanner).
- **Phase 2**: Public Digital Platform (Customer E-Commerce, Online Catalog, Shopping Cart, Customer Account, Ticket & Customer Service Support).
